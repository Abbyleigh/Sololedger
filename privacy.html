Scope and who is responsible

This notice describes the free, browser-only SoloLedger beta and support correspondence. Abbyleigh Boden is responsible for personal information received to operate the website and answer support or privacy requests. You determine why you enter your customers’ information in your own records and must use it lawfully. The app does not send your ledger to SoloLedger or provide a shared customer-record database.

Records entered in the app

Your business profile, payment instructions, customer names and contact/billing details, invoices and transactions are saved in localStorage for this exact website address and browser profile. SoloLedger does not encrypt these records. People or software with access to your device/profile may read them. There is no account, cloud synchronisation, automatic backup or remote recovery. Another device, browser, profile or site address starts separately.

Clearing site data, private browsing, storage eviction, device loss or browser failure may remove your only copy. Use your own protected browser profile and one editing tab. Stale-tab checks are best effort, not transaction locks. Storage capacity varies; the 5 MB JSON safeguard is not a guaranteed browser allowance. A failed save reports an error and retains the previously saved state.

Backups, exports and deletion

Download a private full JSON backup after each session, and check restoration in a separate clean browser profile. JSON includes all app records, including payment instructions; transaction CSV is not a full backup. Restore validates the JSON and replaces all current records after confirmation; it does not merge them. Keep your usual business records separately and use fictional details during the beta.

Business & backups → Delete all local records asks for confirmation before removing this browser’s current and legacy SoloLedger records. It does not delete downloaded JSON/CSV/PDF files, print destinations, device backups or copies held elsewhere. Delete those separately when appropriate. SoloLedger cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local ledger, and browser deletion does not guarantee secure physical erasure. Invoice printing passes details to the browser and the print/PDF destination you choose.

Optional feedback and email support

The feedback form prepares text locally from the answers you provide; it does not read or attach the ledger or submit a report automatically. Downloading keeps the file on your device. Open email draft requests a draft in your chosen mail application; you review it and decide whether to send. Do not send passwords, backups, invoices, customer information or bank details.

If you email the support address, SoloLedger receives your email address, message, any name/signature, optional tester code, browser/device details and any attachments or content you choose to include. The inbox uses Google Gmail. Sending email also involves your chosen email provider and any recipients you add. The app has no mailing-list registration and does not enrol you in marketing or create a subscription.

Why information is used

The proposed basis for minimal support correspondence and website delivery/security information is legitimate interests: answering requests, investigating defects with fictional examples, keeping the website working and protecting it from misuse. Only information necessary for those purposes is intended to be used. Feedback is optional; not sending it does not prevent local testing. There is no solely automated decision-making or profiling producing legal or similarly significant effects.

Retention

Your local records stay until you change/delete them or the browser removes them; SoloLedger imposes no automatic deletion period. You decide how long you must keep your own lawful business records and separate exports.

The support schedule keeps raw correspondence while an issue is active or beta feedback is being assessed. Active cases are reviewed weekly and their continued need is checked monthly; useful findings are reduced to non-identifying issue summaries as soon as practical. Operator-accessible copies are deleted within 30 days after the issue is resolved or the beta ends, whichever is later, unless a documented unresolved request, dispute or legal requirement justifies longer retention. Necessary exceptions are reviewed rather than kept indefinitely. The operator reviews deletion due dates weekly and permanently deletes relevant copies from All Mail (including archived and Sent messages), Drafts, Spam and Trash, plus downloaded attachments and any linked or forwarded copies under the operator’s control, by the deadline. Moving a message to Trash alone does not complete this procedure. This is a manual process, not an app automation. Non-identifying issue summaries may remain only if people cannot reasonably be identified.

Deleting a message does not remove other recipients’ copies. Google and the hosting provider may retain service/security/backup information under their applicable policies; SoloLedger cannot promise immediate deletion from all provider systems. Gmail Trash can retain messages for up to 30 days unless permanently cleared sooner.

The actual account, enabled features and applicable terms are not yet verified, so public deployment remains blocked.

Google’s general consumer terms, privacy and retention information describe its service processing; Google Workspace business data-processing terms, a processor-only role and UK-only storage are not asserted. The support inbox’s access, forwarding, connected applications and local copies still require owner verification. Cloudflare and Google may process information outside the UK. Applicable transfer safeguards, provider roles and retention depend on the service terms and configuration; the owner must confirm the arrangements before publication. Cookies and tracking

SoloLedger’s application sets no cookies and includes no analytics, advertising pixels or third-party scripts. It uses first-party localStorage to retain records you ask it to save. This does not mean hosting collects no technical data. Local release-candidate testing finds no application cookies or external page requests. This is not a test of Cloudflare’s live hosting. Depending on enabled protection, Cloudflare can introduce security cookies such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance and related technical processing. Their presence, purposes and lifetimes must be checked on the actual host before invitations; no blanket cookie-free hosting claim is made. Cloudflare Web Analytics can inject a beacon when enabled, so it and other tracking/injection features must remain off. Any change to tracking or processing requires a fresh assessment and notice update.

Your rights and requests

Contact sololedger.support@gmail.com to request access, correction, erasure or restriction of operator-held personal information, or exercise other rights that apply. Statutory portability applies only where its legal conditions are met; it is not automatically a right for legitimate-interests processing. The app’s full JSON export remains available independently. No identity documents are required with an initial request; identity checks, if needed, are proportionate. Requests normally receive a response within one month, subject to applicable extensions or exceptions. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Your right to object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting the privacy email above. SoloLedger assesses the objection and stops that processing unless lawful grounds permit it to continue. There is no obligation to provide optional feedback. Local records cannot be recovered by a rights request because SoloLedger does not hold a server copy.

A few things to know.

Does this replace my accountant or submit tax returns?

No. SoloLedger currently records income, expenses and non-VAT invoices. It does not calculate tax or submit information to HMRC.

Where are my records stored?

The free beta stores records in your browser, without cloud sync. Export a backup after each session, especially before changing devices or clearing browser data. Records belong to this exact website address and browser profile. There is no automatic backup.

Can I edit an invoice or record a partial payment?

Saved invoices, including drafts, cannot be edited. Check the details before saving; void and recreate an incorrect unpaid invoice. Mark an issued invoice paid only after full payment arrives: it adds one linked income entry and does not collect money. Do not enter that payment again as a transaction. Partial payments and payment reversals are not supported.

What do backup, restore and deletion do?

Business & backups lets you download a full JSON backup. Transaction CSV is not a full backup. Restore validates the JSON and replaces all existing records in this browser after confirmation; it does not merge. Test restores in a separate clean browser profile. Confirmed local deletion removes SoloLedger’s current and legacy records here, but downloaded JSON/CSV/PDF files remain separate.

Can I buy Pro now?

Not yet. The proposed Pro plan is £4.99/month. The proposed upgrade would increase invoice and customer limits; neither plan limits nor paid subscriptions are active. Billing needs approval, implementation and testing before purchase is available.

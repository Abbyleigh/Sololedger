Purpose, scope and price

This free experimental beta is for UK non-VAT sole traders recording income received, expenses paid, customers and simple invoices in GBP. Recorded cash profit is recorded income minus recorded expenses; it is not a tax calculation, bank balance or guarantee of profit. SoloLedger does not calculate VAT, submit HMRC/MTD returns, provide bank feeds or replace your own legal and record-keeping responsibilities.

No account, card, checkout, subscription, paid trial or renewal exists in this beta. Future Free and £4.99/month Pro capacities are proposals only and create no payment obligation. No future charge begins without a separate express agreement.

Your records and customer information

Use fictional data during testing and keep your usual business records separately. You are responsible for the lawfulness and accuracy of the customer and business information you enter, including applicable privacy and retention duties. Do not use the beta as your only copy of required records. Avoid shared or unprotected browser profiles.

Records remain unencrypted in the exact browser profile and website address. There is no cloud sync, automatic backup or remote recovery. Clearing data, private mode, eviction or device loss can erase records. Download private full JSON backups after each session and check restoration in a separate clean profile. CSV contains transactions only. Restore replaces existing records after validation and confirmation; it does not merge.

Invoices, payments and exports

Check legal/trading names, addresses, dates, unique invoice number, descriptions and totals before saving or sharing. Saved invoices, including drafts, cannot be edited. Void and recreate an incorrect unpaid invoice. Paid invoice reversal, part-payments and credit notes are not provided. Mark an issued invoice paid only when full payment arrives: that creates one linked income entry, so do not enter that income again. The app does not collect money or send invoices.

Print / PDF uses your browser’s print options; Save as PDF depends on browser/device support. Review the resulting document. Downloads, print destinations, copies and any external storage services you choose are under your control.

Support, availability and stopping use

Feedback is voluntary and prepared locally; sharing or emailing it is your choice. Send fictional reproduction steps and browser/device information, not real customer records, payment instructions or backups. The support inbox uses Gmail as described in the privacy notice. No guaranteed response time, uptime, compatibility with every device or data recovery is promised.

You can stop testing at any time. Business & backups → Delete all local records requires confirmation and removes the current and legacy SoloLedger records in this browser/profile/site. It does not remove separate exports, backups or copies elsewhere. The operator cannot undo deletion without a valid backup. Report problems and retain independent records rather than continuing a workflow that produces incorrect totals or loses data.
